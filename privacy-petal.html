Privacy Policy

Petal - Period Tracker

Last Updated: March 2026

🌷 Welcome

At Petal, your privacy isn't just important to us - it's the foundation of our app. Petal is a 100% offline period and cycle tracker. Your most personal health data never leaves your device.

This Privacy Policy explains how Petal handles your information.

Our Promise: Petal is completely offline. We do NOT collect, store, transmit, or sell any of your data. Everything stays on your iPhone, always.

📊 Information We Collect

Short answer: Nothing. Petal does not collect any personal data whatsoever.

Data Type Stored Where Sent to Servers
Period & Cycle Data Your device only Never
Mood & Symptoms Your device only Never
Flow & Energy Logs Your device only Never
App Settings Your device only Never
Passcode (if set) Your device only (hashed) Never
Analytics / Tracking Not collected Never

🔒 Data Storage & Security

All your data is stored locally on your device using secure on-device storage:

  • SQLite Database: Period logs, daily entries, and cycle data are stored in a local SQLite database on your iPhone
  • AsyncStorage: App settings and preferences are stored locally
  • No Cloud: There is no server, no cloud backup, and no account system
  • No Network: Petal does not make any network requests (except for In-App Purchase via Apple's StoreKit)

What this means: If you delete the app, all your data is permanently removed. There is no way to recover it because we never had a copy.

🚫 What We Do NOT Do

We want to be absolutely clear:

  • No Data Collection: We collect zero personal data
  • No Tracking: No analytics, no usage tracking, no device fingerprinting
  • No Advertising: No ads, no ad networks, no ad tracking
  • No Third-Party SDKs: No Firebase, no Amplitude, no Mixpanel, no tracking SDKs
  • No Accounts: No sign-up, no login, no email collection
  • No Server Communication: The app works entirely offline
  • No Data Sharing: We cannot share what we don't have

🛒 In-App Purchases

Petal offers an optional one-time In-App Purchase to unlock all features after the free trial period. This purchase is processed entirely by Apple's App Store:

  • Payment is handled by Apple - we never see your payment details
  • Purchase status is stored locally on your device
  • We do not receive any personal financial information

🔔 Notifications

Petal can send you local notifications to remind you about upcoming periods and ovulation:

  • All notifications are local - they are scheduled on your device, not sent from a server
  • No push notification service or token is involved
  • You can disable notifications at any time in the app settings or iOS Settings

👶 Children's Privacy

Petal is designed for individuals who menstruate, typically ages 12 and older. Since we do not collect any data whatsoever, there is no risk of collecting children's personal information. However, this app is not targeted at children under 13.

🌍 Your Rights

Since Petal does not collect, store, or process any personal data on our servers, traditional data rights (access, correction, deletion, portability) are handled directly by you on your device:

  • Access: All your data is visible within the app
  • Deletion: Delete the app to remove all data, or use "Delete All Data" in Settings
  • Portability: Your data lives on your device and is under your control

If you have any questions about your privacy, please contact us below.

  • Significant changes will be communicated through an app update

Our core commitment to being 100% offline and collecting zero data will not change.
